Support

How can we help?

Questions, problems or wrong information? Get in touch, we usually reply within a few days. You can also use Settings → Feedback in the app.

stamp@ihiroshi.com
How do I restore Lifetime Premium?

Open Settings → Restore Purchase while signed in with the Apple ID you bought it with.

What’s free and what’s Premium?

Free includes up to 10 trips and all features, with ads. Lifetime Premium is a one‑time purchase for unlimited trips and no ads, with no subscription.

Where are my trips stored?

On your iPhone. They’re included in your iPhone backup if backup is turned on.

How do I share a trip?

Open a trip and tap Share. Others can import the .stamptrip file into Stamp.

How do I change the app’s language?

Settings → Language in the app opens iOS Settings, where you can pick Stamp’s language.

Data on your device

Your trips, visas and goals, with their dates, notes, photos and files, are stored only on your iPhone and in its backups. We can’t see them.

Anonymous travel statistics

When you add a trip, visa or goal, Stamp sends a small anonymous record to Apple’s iCloud so we can show popular places and improve suggestions: the country or location of a trip, the names and addresses of places and stays, the visa type, and the goal. It never includes your dates, notes, photos, files, name, email or Apple ID. iCloud attaches a random ID that we can’t link to you. You can turn this off anytime in Settings → Share Anonymous Data.

Place search

When you search for a place, your search text is sent to Apple Maps to find results, under Apple’s privacy policy.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device’s advertising identifier, approximate location from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. Ads are personalized only if you allow tracking when iOS asks; otherwise you see non‑personalized ads. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See how Google uses this data. Lifetime Premium removes all ads.

Purchases

Lifetime Premium is bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details.

Feedback

If you send feedback from the app, we receive your message, any screenshot you attach, and your app version, iOS version and language, stored in Apple’s iCloud. We don’t receive your name or email. We use it only to improve Stamp and delete it on request.

Children

Stamp doesn’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
